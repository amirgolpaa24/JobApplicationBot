\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I am writing to apply for the Software Engineer (New Grad), AI Entities position at EvenUp. I recently completed my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, and my experience combines Python backend development, NLP services, and systems for processing large volumes of data. The team's work turning unstructured legal and medical documents into useful, structured information interests me because it brings together two parts of software engineering I enjoy: making messy data usable and building the services that put it to work.

As a Back-End Developer at Asr Gooyesh Pardaz, I developed Django REST APIs for NLP, text-to-speech, and speech-to-text service plans backed by PostgreSQL. I built authentication features, implemented payment-service integration and relational records, and contributed to a WebRTC-based chatbot using GPT-style responses and FAQ data. This gave me practical experience connecting language-processing capabilities with backend functionality that users could access through an application.

My graduate work with BigGeo, Mitacs, and the University of Calgary focused on a different kind of unstructured input: satellite imagery. I built C++/Python pipelines to convert imagery into structured tensors and designed storage and retrieval methods for large spatiotemporal datasets. Through C++ multithreading, I reduced processing time for ten tensors from 233 seconds to 26 seconds; our scaled Canada-wide representation also reduced storage by 38\%. These results came from examining how data was represented and processed, then measuring the effects of implementation choices. That approach would transfer well to improving the performance and scalability of document-processing systems.

I also have hands-on experience with AI beyond backend integration. I built PyTorch training and evaluation workflows for elevation-data super-resolution and am developing an agentic job-search platform with LLM-based matching and skill-gap analysis. The platform integrates Google Sheets, Google Drive, and Telegram, with an initial n8n workflow evolving toward an OpenClaw-based system. These projects have strengthened my interest in the engineering around AI: how inputs are prepared, outputs are evaluated, and workflows connect to other tools.

Collaboration and clear explanation have been central to my work. At the University of Calgary, I supported students working on AWS data pipelines, large-scale processing, and databases. More recently, I mentored an international group through Neuromatch Academy's Deep Learning course. I would bring that communication experience, along with a strong Python foundation and a willingness to learn, to collaboration with EvenUp's engineering, data science, and product teams.

I am drawn to this opportunity because the technical work has a concrete purpose: helping law firms make better use of case information in support of injured people. I would welcome the chance to contribute my backend and data-systems experience while growing into the AI Entities team's document-processing work.

Thank you for considering my application. I look forward to discussing how my experience could contribute to EvenUp.
