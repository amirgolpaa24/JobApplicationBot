\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I am writing to apply for the Data Engineer role at Lime. With a Master of Science in Computer Science from the University of Calgary and hands-on experience building Python/C++ data-processing software, SQL-backed backend services, ETL-style pipelines, and scalable workflows for large real-world datasets, I am excited by the opportunity to help build data infrastructure that supports analytics, machine learning, and real-time business intelligence for a global micromobility platform.

I automated SAR and optical imagery preprocessing using GDAL, Rasterio, SNAP, and external data APIs, and developed DGGS-based ETL-style pipelines that transformed imagery into structured tensor files for analytics and machine learning workflows. This work gave me practical experience with data ingestion, transformation, performance, reproducibility, data quality, and documentation.

That background aligns well with Lime's need for robust data pipelines, performance-tuned transformations, reliable datasets, and close collaboration with analytics and ML teams. I have strong experience with Python, SQL, PostgreSQL-backed systems, Git-based development, Docker, Linux, and AWS-based data coursework. I also have experience preparing clean, feature-rich datasets for model training and evaluation through my DEM super-resolution work, where I built data collection and preprocessing workflows and evaluated model outputs with statistical and terrain-aware metrics.

I have not yet worked with Snowflake, Airflow, dbt, Spark, Flink, or Kafka in a production environment, but the core engineering pattern is familiar to me: reason carefully about schemas and access patterns, build reproducible pipelines, validate outputs, optimize bottlenecks, document decisions, and make data usable for downstream teams. I would bring strong learning habits, a careful software engineering mindset, and relevant hands-on experience with large-scale data processing, backend APIs, relational databases, and ML-oriented datasets.

I am particularly interested in Lime because the work connects data infrastructure with a concrete public-facing mission: making shared, affordable, lower-carbon transportation work at scale. The idea of building reliable data systems behind fleet analytics, operational intelligence, and ML models is very motivating to me. I would be excited to contribute to a remote Canadian engineering team where data quality, observability, stewardship, and scalable systems all matter.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my Python, SQL, backend, cloud-data coursework, large-scale data-processing, and machine learning data preparation experience can contribute to Lime's Data Engineering team.
