\opening{Dear Hiring Team,}

I am writing to apply for the Data Scientist, North Insights position at Cohere. I am especially excited by the role because it sits close to the questions I enjoy most: how to measure whether AI systems are truly useful, how to turn ambiguous product questions into rigorous analyses, and how to communicate evidence clearly enough that teams can act on it.

My background combines machine learning, data engineering, and research communication. During my M.Sc. in Computer Science at the University of Calgary, I built Python and C++ systems for large geospatial and Earth observation datasets, including pipelines for data collection, preprocessing, model training, validation, storage, retrieval, and performance analysis. In my DEM super-resolution project, I trained PyTorch models, evaluated them with terrain-aware metrics, and found that random data splitting created overly optimistic results. I then designed region-based validation methods that gave a more honest view of real-world generalization.

That experience maps well to the kind of ownership described in the North Insights role. I am comfortable taking a broad question, defining the data and metrics needed to answer it, building the model or analysis, and explaining the tradeoffs behind the result. My published DGGS research also strengthened my ability to reason about scalability, data quality, and retrieval performance, while my backend experience gave me practical exposure to SQL, PostgreSQL, APIs, Git-based development, and production-oriented engineering habits.

Cohere's focus on enterprise AI is also personally compelling to me. I have been following how LLMs are moving from impressive demos into tools that need evaluation, reliability, user trust, and measurable value. The chance to help measure adoption, product impact, customer value, and AI behavior for Cohere's North product feels like a strong match for both my technical background and my curiosity about applied AI systems.

I do not want to overstate areas where I am still growing: I have less direct experience with GTM analytics, large-scale A/B testing, and tools such as BigQuery, dbt, Looker, and Airflow than someone coming from a pure product analytics role. However, I bring strong Python, modeling, validation, data-pipeline, and research judgment, and I learn quickly when the problem is meaningful. I would be excited to bring that combination to Cohere and contribute to analytics that help product, research, sales, and customers understand what is actually working.

Thank you for considering my application. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in machine learning, model evaluation, and large-scale data systems can contribute to the North Insights team.
