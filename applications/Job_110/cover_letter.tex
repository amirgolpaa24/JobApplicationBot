I am writing to apply for the Data Scientist contract role with Systematix. With a Master of Science in Computer Science from the University of Calgary and hands-on experience in Python, large-scale data processing, predictive modelling, model evaluation, documentation, and technical communication, I am excited by the opportunity to contribute to your client's optimization and predictive modelling initiative.

My graduate research focused on scalable machine learning and data-management workflows for large geospatial and Earth observation datasets. That experience is directly relevant to building models that are accurate, understandable, stable, and defensible.

These projects strengthened my understanding of data architecture, ETL-style workflows, performance analysis, reproducibility, and the practical challenges of making large datasets usable for downstream modelling.

The role's focus on mixed-integer linear programming and optimization solvers is an area where I would be building further depth, but the surrounding responsibilities match my background strongly: Python, Pandas-style data work, predictive modelling, large datasets, debugging analytical behavior, documenting assumptions, and communicating methods and trade-offs clearly. I have also worked with SQL, PostgreSQL, Docker, Git, Linux, AWS-based coursework, and backend APIs, which gives me a practical engineering foundation for maintainable data science workflows.

I also bring experience explaining complex analytical concepts to different audiences. As a teaching assistant at the University of Calgary and a Deep Learning Teaching Assistant with Neuromatch Academy, I supported students through programming, data, and machine learning workflows, reviewed project work, and helped teams reason through ambiguity.
