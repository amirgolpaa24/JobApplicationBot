I am writing to apply for the Machine Learning Scientist (12 month term) position with Amii. The professional accomplishment I am most proud of is my first-author publication in Remote Sensing on real-time multiresolution management of spatiotemporal Earth observation data using DGGS. I am proud of it because it represents several years of independent research, engineering, experimentation, writing, revision, and collaboration brought to a peer-reviewed result. Beyond research, I bring mentoring and technical communication experience. I would bring Amii a strong foundation in applied ML, Python engineering, model evaluation, scientific communication, and responsible experimentation.
