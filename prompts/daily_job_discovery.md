## Active-Posting Verification

Never add a job without checking whether applications are still being accepted.

Use this verification order:

1. Employer's official career or application page
2. Employer's applicant-tracking-system page
3. LinkedIn posting
4. Another reliable job board only when stronger sources are unavailable

A job is inactive and must not be added when any of the following applies:

- HTTP 404 or a genuine job-not-found page
- "No longer accepting applications"
- "Position filled," "job closed," "expired," "removed," or equivalent wording
- The URL redirects only to a general careers homepage or search page and the specific job cannot be found
- The application deadline has passed
- The Apply button is disabled
- Clicking Apply does not lead to a functioning application entry point
- The employer's authoritative page says the role is unavailable, even if an aggregator still displays it

When the direct employer link is active, use it as the Direct Application Link.

If the employer page is temporarily inaccessible because of bot protection but the posting is very recent and LinkedIn clearly shows that applications remain open, the job may be added. Explain the verification limitation briefly in Notes.

Do not fabricate a direct application URL.

## Fit-Score Method

Read the complete responsibilities and requirements. Do not calculate fit from the title or keyword count alone.

Score each posting from 0 to 100 using:

- 0-30: Match between Amir's experience and the actual daily responsibilities
- 0-25: Match to required technical skills
- 0-15: Realistic seniority and years-of-experience alignment
- 0-10: Domain relevance, such as AI, ML, data, geospatial, scientific software, backend, cloud, automation, or research
- 0-10: Canadian location and work-arrangement suitability
- 0-5: Education, publication, teaching, and research alignment
- 0-5: Strength of transferable skills and realistic ability to close remaining gaps

Apply meaningful deductions for mandatory gaps. Do not deduct heavily for optional or preferred qualifications.

Use these calibration bands:

- 90-100: Exceptional direct match
- 80-89: Very strong match
- 70-79: Strong and realistic match
- 60-69: Plausible match with manageable gaps
- 50-59: Adjacent or transferable opportunity
- Below 50: Normally exclude

To avoid missing promising early-career opportunities, a score of 45-49 may still be added when all of the following are true:

- It is junior, graduate, internship, associate, or otherwise early-career.
- At least two meaningful parts of Amir's background match.
- No hard eligibility blocker exists.
- Notes clearly explain why it was included despite the lower score.

Do not inflate scores to justify adding a job. Comparable jobs should receive comparable scores.

## Priority Method

Priority must reflect recency first, then fit, while also considering location, level, and urgency.

Calculate an internal Priority Index:

`Priority Index = (Fit Score x 0.60) + Recency Points + Location Points + Level/Urgency Points`

Recency Points:

- Posted within 24 hours: 30
- Posted 2-3 days ago: 25
- Posted 4-7 days ago: 18
- Posted 8-14 days ago: 10
- Posted 15-30 days ago: 5
- Older than 30 days: 0

Location Points:

- Calgary: 10
- Remote throughout Canada: 9
- Edmonton or elsewhere in Alberta: 8
- British Columbia: 6
- Ontario: 5
- Elsewhere in Canada: 4

Level/Urgency Points, maximum 5:

- Junior, new-grad, graduate, associate, internship, or early-career: up to 5
- Application deadline within five days: up to 5
- Realistically attainable intermediate role: up to 3

Assign:

- High: Priority Index 75 or higher
- Mid: Priority Index 58-74
- Low: Priority Index below 58

Use judgment when reliable posting dates are unavailable, but never invent a date.
